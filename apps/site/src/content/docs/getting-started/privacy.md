The AbsoluteUI website (home page, documentation, and Theme Designer) has one [Privacy Policy](/privacy/). This page summarizes what applies to the documentation and lets you change your analytics choice.

## Analytics

The website uses **Microsoft Clarity** to understand how it is used. Clarity loads only after you choose **Accept analytics** in the consent banner. Until then, nothing is sent to Clarity and no analytics cookie is set. With your consent, Clarity records page views and interactions such as clicks and scrolling, and sets the cookies `_clck` and `_clsk`. Advertising storage is always denied.

## Your choice

Your choice is stored in this browser and applies to the whole website: the home page, the documentation, and the Theme Designer. To change it, reset it here and choose again in the banner. Resetting also removes Clarity's cookies.

<button type="button" data-consent-reset>Change analytics choice</button>

You can also use **Cookie settings** in the footer of any page.

## Stored in your browser

The documentation also keeps your theme, your live-example settings, your last code tab, and which sidebar groups are open in your browser. None of it is sent to us. The [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) lists each item.

## The AbsoluteUI library

The AbsoluteUI packages send no analytics or telemetry, and components never write user input to storage or cookies. See [Security and CSP](/docs/getting-started/security/).
