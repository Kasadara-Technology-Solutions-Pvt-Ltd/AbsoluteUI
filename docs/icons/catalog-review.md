## Why the total is below 450

The catalog was not padded to 25 per category. Synonyms became aliases, slash and badge states became compositions, and rotations became render options. Categories under 25 (Users, Files, Time, Security, Enterprise, Development, Cloud, Finance, Maps, Media, Communication) are listed with candidates in the plan (section 4.3); adding them is a user decision (R16-8), not a quota to fill.

## Consolidated (merged into one canonical icon)

- `copy`: duplicate merged into copy (no visual distinction at 16px).
- `printer`: Merged: the print action and the printer device are the same icon.
- `trophy`: award (medal) merged into trophy.
- `users`: team (three people) merged: not distinct at 16px.
- `clipboard`: paste merged: the paste action uses the clipboard.
- `chart-bar`: Horizontal bars are a rotation; histogram merged (adjacent bars not distinct at 16px).
- `chart-pie`: Donut merged into pie.
- `org-chart`: sitemap merged: same tree metaphor.

## Not catalog entries

- Overlays (composition marks, bottom-end anchor): `plus` (Add), `minus` (Remove), `check` (Done or approved), `x` (Failed or removed), `alert` (Needs attention), `lock` (Locked), `clock` (Scheduled or pending), `star` (Featured), `arrow-up` (Upload or increase), `arrow-down` (Download or decrease), `search` (Find), `settings` (Configure), `dot` (Unread or status dot).
- Containers: `circle`, `square` (e.g. `folder` in the circle container; `user-circle` and `plus-circle` are drawn as their own icons, R16-8).
- Rotations and flips: rotate-ccw, horizontal chart bars, end and bottom panels, horizontal grip.
- Excluded marks: Bluetooth, USB, contactless, payment networks, platform share and face-ID symbols, file-format letters (PDF, XLS): trademarks or vendor marks.
