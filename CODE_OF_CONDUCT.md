# Code of Conduct

This code applies to everyone who takes part in AbsoluteUI's spaces: this repository's issues, pull requests, and security advisories, and any other channel Kasadara Technology Solutions Pvt Ltd runs for the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding.
- Keep discussion on the topic: the software, its documentation, and its use.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respect people's time: search before asking, and provide the details needed to help you.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct based on any personal characteristic, such as age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, race, religion, sexual orientation, or level of experience.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Trolling, spam, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Disclosing a security vulnerability publicly before a fix is released (see the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md)).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, lock or close issues, and block accounts that break this code, with or without a warning, depending on how serious the behavior is.

Report unacceptable behavior to **legalops@kasadara.com**, or with GitHub's **Report content** option. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially; the reporter's identity is not shared without their consent.

## Scope

This code applies within the project's spaces, and when someone represents the project in public.
