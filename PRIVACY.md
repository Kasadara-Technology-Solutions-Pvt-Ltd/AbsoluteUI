## 3. What we collect, and why

### 3.1 Analytics, only with your consent

The Sites use **Microsoft Clarity** (one Clarity project for all three sections) to understand how pages are used, so we can improve them. Clarity stays off until you choose **Accept analytics** in the banner. One choice applies to all three sections. Until then, no request is made to Clarity and no analytics cookie is set.

With your consent, Clarity records page views and interactions such as clicks, scrolling, and mouse movement, along with technical data such as browser, device type, approximate location derived from your IP address, and the referring page. It sets the first-party cookies `_clck` and `_clsk`. Text you type into form fields is masked by Clarity by default. Advertising storage is always denied.

- **Why:** to improve the Sites.
- **Legal basis:** your consent. You can withdraw it at any time (section 6).
- **Who processes it:** Microsoft Corporation, as our processor, under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).


### 3.2 Delivering the Sites

To deliver pages, our hosting provider, Microsoft Azure (Azure Static Web Apps), necessarily processes your IP address and request details. We do not add tracking to these requests and we do not combine them with other data.

- **Why:** to deliver the Sites and keep them secure.
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in operating secure websites; under the DPDP Act, a legitimate use for providing the service you request.

### 3.3 Stored only in your browser

These items are kept in your browser's storage. They are not sent to us.

| Site | Name | Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| All three sections | `aui-site-analytics-consent` | local storage | Your analytics choice for the whole website. |
| Product site | `aui-site-theme` | local storage | Light or dark theme, if you switch it. |
| Documentation | `starlight-theme` | local storage | Light, dark, or automatic theme, if you change it. |
| Documentation | `aui-live-settings` | local storage | Theme, density, direction, and locale you pick for live examples. |
| Documentation | `starlight-synced-tabs__*` | local storage | The code tab you last picked, kept in sync across pages. |
| Documentation | `sl-sidebar-state` | session storage | Which sidebar groups are open, until you close the tab. |
| Theme Designer | `aui-theme-designer-draft` | local storage | Your unsaved theme draft, so a reload does not lose it. |
| All three sections | `_clck`, `_clsk` | cookies (Microsoft Clarity) | Set only after you accept analytics. |

The Theme Designer runs entirely in your browser. The colors, names, and files you create there are not sent to us. If you add a web font from Google Fonts or Bunny Fonts to a theme, the preview loads that font from the provider you chose, which receives your IP address under its own privacy policy.

### 3.4 Messages you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and anything you attach. We use them to reply and, for security reports, to investigate and to credit you in an advisory if you agree.

- **Legal basis:** your consent, given by contacting us, and our legitimate interest in handling security reports.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or make automated decisions about you.

## 4. How long we keep data

- **Analytics:** Microsoft keeps Clarity data for the periods set out in Clarity's documentation; we do not keep separate copies.
- **Browser storage:** stays in your browser until you clear it or, for withdrawal of analytics consent, until you change your choice.
- **Messages:** kept as long as needed to handle your request, and for security reports, for as long as the related advisory is published.

## 5. Where data is processed

Microsoft may process Clarity and hosting data outside India, including in the United States. Where the GDPR applies, Microsoft relies on its own transfer safeguards, described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

- **Change your analytics choice:** use **Cookie settings** in the footer of any of the Sites. Withdrawing consent removes the Clarity cookies and reloads the page. The choice applies to the whole website; it is kept per address, so https://absoluteui.kasadara.com keeps its own.
- **Your rights:** depending on the law that applies to you, you can ask to access, correct, complete, update, or erase your personal data; withdraw consent; nominate another person to exercise your rights; object to or restrict processing; and receive a copy of your data. Email legalops@kasadara.com. We will respond within the time the applicable law requires.
- **Grievances:** send privacy concerns to legalops@kasadara.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India or, where the GDPR applies, to your local data protection authority.

Much of the data in section 3.1 is held by Microsoft and is not linked to your name, so we may be unable to identify which records relate to you.
